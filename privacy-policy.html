Softpix Studio ("we", "our", or "us") develops and publishes mobile games. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, and shared when you use our mobile games.

Our mobile games may automatically collect certain non-personal information, including:

Some of our mobile games use Google AdMob to display advertisements.

For more information, visit Google Privacy Policy .

Information collected through our mobile games may be used for:
